b or d Mouth Check

A small-group check for students who mix up b and d. The adult asks the mouth and sound questions and marks each turn.

Names stay on this page. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Copy the results before you close the page.

Each student gets an equal number of b and d cards (one extra of a random letter if the number of turns is odd).

Ask the student

  1. Straight line or open?
  2. Sound?

Shortcuts: C correct, N not yet, U undo.
